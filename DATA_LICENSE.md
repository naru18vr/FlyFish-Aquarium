## Modifications

The application selects 768 CX / DNa01 / DNa02 neurons, prioritizing compass/PFL types and then total internal synapse strength. It takes the induced directed graph, sums counts across neuropils, excludes self connections and edges with fewer than five summed synapses, and retains original neuron root IDs as strings. This produces 25,274 directed connections. The 256 / 512 / 768 quality levels use nested prefixes of this graph.

The raw synapse counts, predicted neurotransmitter labels, neuron IDs, and anatomical type labels are preserved. Numerical simulation normalizes incoming weights and treats GABA and glutamate as inhibitory; other/unknown types are simplified to excitatory. `adapterGroup` is an artificial application-specific projection/readout assignment, **not** an anatomical or experimentally established fish-control annotation.

Raw-file SHA-256 hashes and exact extraction rules are embedded in `connectome.json`. `scripts/extract_connectome.py` reproduces the derivative without third-party Python libraries. The bundled graph is a truncated model and does not reproduce the whole brain or validate biological behavior.
